# ADR0011: Bounded, complete authority decision bundles

Status: accepted for the pure reference engine, 2026-11-16. Protected runtime storage, canonical loaders, compiled-filter equivalence and atomic ingress activation remain separate required gates.

A decision must consider every relevant allow, deny, issuer and prerequisite under one tenant snapshot and one cumulative budget. Evaluating a filtered grant list or trusting a caller's `issuerAllowed` flag could conceal a deny or retain revoked delegation. Field reads and workers also need exact provenance without inheriting general resource authority.

We adopt the [decision contract](../execution/SCOPED_AUTHORITY_DECISION_CONTRACT.md) and its preserved candidate. Public entrypoints admit unknown input, validate closed records and exact immutable policy/role/type pins, derive complete grant chains and evaluate an acyclic graph once per node. All applicable predicates use the existing three-valued interpreter. Indeterminate evidence prevents allow; explicit deny wins. Ordinary field compatibility derives only from every registered non-field prerequisite. Sensitive fields require separate explicit authority. A resource-wide deny guard can refuse access but never grant it.

Pure human, field and service contexts bind exact subject, request, tenant, revision, currentness, facts, ceiling records and protected operation companions. Live issuers require their own complete current decisions; ordinary historical issuers are provenance only. Worker child leases attenuate the root site's immutable job/claim, audience, credential, assignment, actions and interval. Safe terminalization remains independent of expired metadata leases. Journal append requires an exact unconsumed claim and all parent decisions. Applied projection events require the exact action/request commitment and replay mode.

The bundle shares finite physical, semantic, dependency, witness and retained-canonical-byte limits. Dependency depth16 permits four delegation edges with deny guards and field prerequisites. Output is complete within its bound; failure returns no partial decisions. Future validity changes, including currently ineffective candidates and denied dependencies, bound the exclusive horizon. These bounds are not heap measurements, an operation-wide quota or a measured30-second capacity guarantee.

Identity helpers are construction tools, not signatures or capabilities. Pure input cannot authenticate a producer, discover omitted database rows, prove native-source privileges, fence SQL effects or authorize disclosure of explanations. Protected storage must enforce these properties and independently gate every HTTP, search, worker, replay and mutation path. The existing runtime stays in force until the complete cutover passes its real gates; pure-engine success does not qualify the full requirement, a deployment profile or release.
