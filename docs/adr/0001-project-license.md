# ADR 0001: Apache-2.0 original distribution and DCO contributions

Date: 2026-11-15. Status: accepted by the governing build specification.
Requirements: OSS-001, OSS-002, DIF-012.

The original platform, SDKs, connector framework, deployment code and project
documentation use Apache-2.0. The unmodified official text is in LICENSE.
Contributors retain their rights and attribution; NOTICE does not invent a legal
copyright owner or imply Apache Software Foundation sponsorship.

This supports independent commercial use, enterprise adoption, hosted operation
and extensions while retaining notice obligations and the contributor patent
grant. A proprietary derivative may be distributed or hosted without a general
requirement to publish all modifications. Apache's trademark terms do not grant
rights to comparison vendors' marks. Required product features stay in the open
repository without artificial premium, seat, asset or connector license gates.

AGPL-3.0 was considered as the alternative for an explicit reciprocal source
goal: Section 13 requires a modified network-served version to offer its
corresponding source to interacting remote users. It does not prohibit commercial
use or automatically require every adjacent service to disclose source. Its
combined-work scope needs analysis. It is not selected, and future contributions
cannot simply be relicensed without the necessary rights.

Use the unmodified DCO 1.1 text and genuine contributor certifications. DCO does
not transfer copyright. Never manufacture human sign-offs for generated work.
Public metadata persists; contributors may use a verified configured noreply
identity. Original synthetic fixtures remain independent of employers/clients.

Third-party libraries, containers, tools, documents, fonts, drivers and model
weights retain their actual licenses. Review exact versions, SPDX expressions,
redistribution context and obligations. Nonredistributable drivers are supplied
separately by the operator; they cannot become core local prerequisites.

Revisit only if the user changes the distribution goal and a contribution-rights
and dependency migration plan is approved. Do not silently change licensing.
